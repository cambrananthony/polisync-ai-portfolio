# PoliSync AI — AI-Assisted Policy Management Platform

## Project Overview

PoliSync is an independent software project focused on simplifying how businesses create, manage and maintain organisational policies.

The platform explores how artificial intelligence can reduce repetitive administrative work, improve access to policy information and help organisations manage documentation more efficiently.

## Problem Being Addressed

Small and medium-sized businesses often face challenges creating and maintaining policies, particularly when they lack dedicated compliance or governance teams.

PoliSync aims to make policy management more accessible through an intuitive, technology-enabled platform.

## Technical Approach

The application has been developed using a combination of modern web technologies, including:

- JavaScript and TypeScript
- Python
- Next.js
- PostgreSQL

The project provides an opportunity to explore the application of AI to document creation, enterprise workflows and policy management.

## AI Safety and Reliability Considerations

PoliSync highlights important questions surrounding the responsible use of AI-generated business documentation, including:

- How can AI-generated policies be evaluated for accuracy and completeness?
- How can organisations maintain appropriate human oversight?
- How should sensitive business information be protected?
- How can AI systems communicate uncertainty and limitations?

These are areas I am interested in exploring further through practical AI evaluation and safety research.
